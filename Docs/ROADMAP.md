# SakuraDays roadmap

## Milestone 01 — A playable spring street

The player spawns on a short Japanese neighborhood street lined with sakura trees, walks to a café, and speaks with one NPC while petals fall. The café entrance or frontage serves as the interaction point.

| Increment | Playable outcome |
|---|---|
| Street blockout | The player can spawn, walk the full route, and reach the café. Street boundaries and collision guide the route. |
| Café and NPC | The café is easy to recognize from the street. One NPC stands near the entrance and shows an interaction prompt when approached. |
| Dialogue | The player can start, advance, and finish a short conversation, then walk away or start it again. |
| Sakura and petals | Trees frame the street and falling petals are visible along the route without obscuring the NPC or dialogue. |
| Playtest and polish | A fresh Play In Editor session completes the whole loop without a blocker. Check collision, prompt visibility, dialogue dismissal, and petal visibility from the player camera. |

Milestone scope ends at the café conversation. Additional NPCs, a café interior, branching dialogue, and a packaged release can be planned after this loop is playable.
